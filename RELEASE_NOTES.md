# v1.12.0-bphenriques.1

Based on [pvaibhav's slot v1.12.0-pvaibhav.2](https://github.com/pvaibhav/slot/releases/tag/v1.12.0-pvaibhav.2), itself built on [upstream slot 1.12.0](https://github.com/BrandonKowalski/slot/releases/tag/v1.12.0), with a music and audiobook player built in, [mute](https://github.com/bphenriques/mute-player), under Music in the quick menu.

- **From slot 1.12.0:** game guides (a `.txt` named after the game in `Guides/GBA`, `Guides/GB` or `Guides/GBC`, then Guide in the in-game menu); the Shimmerless shader; Saturation and Contrast; a Reset Game shortcut, with Undo; Boktai's solar sensor from the clock, with Sunlight Up and Down shortcuts; bezels made for one Scale by a `_spscreen`, `_integer` or `_fit` name ending; and SP Frame is now SP Screen. Upstream's changelog has the rest.
- **From pvaibhav's fork:** the Authentic shader, drawing each handheld's reflective LCD (with a Model row on Game Boy: Game Boy, Pocket or Light); the CPU governor chosen by core, `schedutil` for Game Boy and paused play and `performance` for GBA on mGBA; cheats, turned on and off from the in-game menu (MENU › Cheats, for a game with cheats; the card now comes with `Cheats.pack` from [pvaibhav's release](https://github.com/pvaibhav/slot/releases/tag/v1.12.0-pvaibhav.2), and your own `.cht` files go in `Cheats/`); a holographic seal on carts that can earn RetroAchievements offline, and its automatic 120 Hz, display calibration, home Wi-Fi and linking, label downloads and RetroAchievements.
- **Library:** put songs in `Music/` on the card, any layout; or anywhere else, as on the other card (`music_folder` and `books_folder` in `music.txt`); tags win, folders fill gaps. Library has Artists, Albums and Songs, Genres and Years once the tags name them, and Folders as they are on the card; compilations are kept as one album. Playlists (`.m3u` in `Music/Playlists/`), Favorites, Recently Played and Recently Added. Tags are read once and cached, so the library opens at once afterwards.
- **Playing:** gapless; Crossfade only between albums, so an album played in order stays gapless; Volume Leveling from ReplayGain; an equalizer (Settings › Music) with slot's presets and your own, made on the device, heard as you set them, and renamed or kept in `Config/music-eq.txt`; Mono; and, if you like, a Visualizer: a faint level meter beside the picture, moving with the music. Synced lyrics from `.lrc` files or tags, followed with the lines around fading and the song's progress above, played from any line. The queue says how long it has left; Folders show their albums' pictures. Play Next and Add to Queue as Spotify has them; the queue, its place and its shuffle come back after a restart.
- **Audiobooks:** from `Audiobooks/`, a folder or an `.m4b` a book. Each keeps its place, speed (0.5× to 2×, the voice at its pitch) and bookmarks; chapters from `.m4b` files, MP3s' chapters or a folder's files; it picks up a little back after a pause, the longer the further; Skip Silence and Volume Boost; the sleep timer stops at the end of a chapter, going back two minutes; In Progress and Finished lists.
- **Keys:** a tap does one thing and a hold another, as the hints say ("Play (Next)"), a ring filling while one is held. Settings › Help, shown on the first start, lists the keys the hints leave out. With Screen Off set to Keep Playing, music goes on with the lid closed and the shoulders still skip, seek and pause; two shoulders held lock the keys for a pocket, or Play Locked locks them whenever the lid closes.
- **Card:** formats are MP3, AAC and Apple Lossless (`.m4a`, `.m4b`), FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV. A card with nothing on it says where songs and books go; a write the card refuses says "Can't save".

This is a preview for feedback. Music is decoded with Symphonia (MPL-2.0), resampled with Rubato, tagged with Lofty, and played at another speed with SoundTouch (LGPL-2.1, built from its sources); Opus is decoded with libopus (BSD-3-Clause, built from its sources).

Unzip, then copy the contents of `slot-v1.12.0-bphenriques.1/` onto SD2. Your saves and settings are preserved. A card from before keeps an unused `System/muse/` and `System/muse.sh`, safe to delete.

For labels without Wi-Fi, copy `Artwork.pack` into `Labels/` on your SD card. Cart images in `Artwork.pack` come from the [LaunchBox Games Database](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com) and its contributors. Label artwork belongs to each game's publisher. The pack is free for personal use; please don't sell it.

Cheats are from the [libretro database](https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database) and its contributors, under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Emulation by mGBA (MPL-2.0) and gpSP (GPL-2.0). Licences, patches, and gpSP source are in `System/licenses/`: SoundTouch's in `soundtouch-LGPL-2.1.txt`, and every crate the music player links, with where its source is, in `slot-music-third-party.txt`.
