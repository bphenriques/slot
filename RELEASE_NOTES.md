# v1.15.3-bphenriques.1

Based on [pvaibhav's slot v1.15.3-pvaibhav.1](https://github.com/pvaibhav/slot/releases/tag/v1.15.3-pvaibhav.1), itself built on [upstream slot 1.15.3](https://github.com/slot-cfw/slot/releases/tag/v1.15.3), with a music and audiobook player built in, [mute](https://github.com/bphenriques/mute-player), under Music in the quick menu.

- **From slot 1.13.0 to 1.15.3:** Collections, grouping games across systems (hold Y on a game); Kid Mode; Resume on Insert and Resume on Power On; a regrouped settings menu, with the battery shown as an icon, a percentage or both; Network in the shelf menu for Wi-Fi; System › Check for Updates, which installs this fork's releases and never upstream's or pvaibhav's; Gamebit, for managing the card from a browser at `slot.local`; Cart Studio and Fetch Missing Art; network time; the Per Shelf wallpaper; Gambatte, a new Game Boy core and the default on new cards (cheats need mGBA: START on a Game Boy cart switches it); X on the shelf takes a game off Recents; and six lower brightness steps. [Upstream's changelog](https://github.com/slot-cfw/slot/blob/v1.15.3/CHANGELOG.md) has the rest, and 1.12.0's guides, Shimmerless, Saturation and Contrast, and Reset Game.
- **From pvaibhav's fork:** the Authentic shader (with a Reflection setting for its glass), drawing each handheld's reflective LCD (with a Model row on Game Boy: Game Boy, Pocket or Light), now in lens bezels from Harlequin's border art, with truer GBA colours and deeper dot shadows; the CPU governor chosen by core, `schedutil` for Game Boy and paused play and `performance` for GBA on mGBA; cheats, turned on and off from the in-game menu (MENU › Cheats, for a game with cheats; the card now comes with `Cheats.pack` from [pvaibhav's releases](https://github.com/pvaibhav/slot/releases), and your own `.cht` files go in `Cheats/`); a holographic seal on carts that can earn RetroAchievements offline; GPU frame blending under every shader and core; and his automatic 120 Hz, display calibration and RetroAchievements, with Wi-Fi now kept through a game so unlocks reach the site as you play. His home Wi-Fi, home-network linking and label downloads give way to upstream's Network menu, direct link and Fetch Missing Art.
- **Library:** put songs in `Music/` on the card, any layout; or anywhere else, as on the other card (`music_folder` and `books_folder` in `music.txt`); tags win, folders fill gaps. Library has Artists, Albums and Songs, Genres and Years once the tags name them, and Folders as they are on the card; compilations are kept as one album. Playlists (`.m3u` in `Music/Playlists/`), Favorites, Recently Played and Recently Added. Tags are read once and cached, so the library opens at once afterwards.
- **Playing:** gapless; Crossfade only between albums, so an album played in order stays gapless; Volume Leveling from ReplayGain; an equalizer (Settings › Music) with slot's presets and your own, made on the device, heard as you set them, and renamed or kept in `Config/music-eq.txt`; Mono; and, if you like, a Visualizer: a faint level meter beside the picture, moving with the music. Synced lyrics from `.lrc` files or tags, followed with the lines around fading and the song's progress above, played from any line. The queue says how long it has left; Folders show their albums' pictures. Play Next and Add to Queue as Spotify has them; the queue, its place and its shuffle come back after a restart.
- **Audiobooks:** from `Audiobooks/`, a folder or an `.m4b` a book. Each keeps its place, speed (0.5× to 2×, the voice at its pitch) and bookmarks; chapters from `.m4b` files, MP3s' chapters or a folder's files; it picks up a little back after a pause, the longer the further; Skip Silence and Volume Boost; the sleep timer stops at the end of a chapter, going back two minutes; In Progress and Finished lists, a book begun showing a bar and the time it has left. Left and Right jump 15 seconds, as Spotify does; Jump Length offers 10 or 30.
- **Keys:** a tap does one thing and a hold another, as the hints say ("Play (Next)"), a ring filling while one is held. Settings › Help, shown on the first start, lists the keys the hints leave out. With Screen Off set to Keep Playing, music goes on with the lid closed and the shoulders still skip, seek and pause; two shoulders held lock the keys for a pocket, or Play Locked locks them whenever the lid closes.
- **Card:** formats are MP3, AAC and Apple Lossless (`.m4a`, `.m4b`), FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV. A card with nothing on it says where songs and books go; a write the card refuses says "Can't save".

This is a preview for feedback. Music is decoded with Symphonia (MPL-2.0), resampled with Rubato, tagged with Lofty, and played at another speed with SoundTouch (LGPL-2.1, built from its sources); Opus is decoded with libopus (BSD-3-Clause, built from its sources).

Unzip, then copy the contents of `slot-v1.15.3-bphenriques.1/` onto SD2. Your saves and settings are preserved. Join your Wi-Fi again from Network in the shelf menu; slot deletes the old `Config/wifi.toml` and the `slot-services` and `slot-net` files on its first start. A card from before keeps an unused `System/muse/` and `System/muse.sh`, safe to delete.

For labels without Wi-Fi, copy `Artwork.pack`, beside the zip in this release, into `Labels/` on your SD card. Cart images in `Artwork.pack` come from the [LaunchBox Games Database](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com) and its contributors. Label artwork belongs to each game's publisher. The pack is free for personal use; please don't sell it.

Cheats are from the [libretro database](https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database) and its contributors, under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Emulation by mGBA (MPL-2.0), gpSP (GPL-2.0) and Gambatte (GPL-2.0). Licences, patches, and the gpSP and Gambatte sources are in `System/licenses/`: SoundTouch's in `soundtouch-LGPL-2.1.txt`, and every crate the music player links, with where its source is, in `slot-music-third-party.txt`.
