# Cheats

Put Cheats.pack from the release page in this folder. While a game is
running, press MENU and choose Cheats to turn its cheats on or off.

To use your own cheats for a game, add a RetroArch .cht file to GBA, GBAV, GB or
GBC, named after the ROM. It replaces that game's cheats from the pack.

  Games/GBA/Metroid Fusion.gba  ->  Cheats/GBA/Metroid Fusion.cht

Cheats work with the mGBA core. A cheat that changes items or money can
end up in your save, even after you turn it off.

Cheats.pack holds cheats from the libretro database
(https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database) by its contributors, matched
to No-Intro dumps by slot. It is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
